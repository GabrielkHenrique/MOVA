# MOVA UX & Home Concept

## Design Principles
1. **Videogame First:** The patient experiences a playful, charming world, not a medical dashboard.
2. **Director and Actor:** The therapist controls the flow (director), the patient experiences the adventure (actor).
3. **Accessibility:** Large touch targets, high contrast, clear focus states, predictable navigation. No tiny buttons or complex menus.
4. **The "Wii Vibe":** Simple navigation, satisfying visual and audio feedback, satisfying transitions, and clear world metaphors.

## Chosen Concept: MOVA Plaza (The World's Fair / Theme Park)
We have selected a variation of the "World's Fair / Park" concept. 
- **Visual Idea:** A warm, colorful illustrated park/plaza. Each available game is represented by a physical structure or landmark (e.g., a balloon stand for "Estoura-Balões").
- **Why it fits:** It perfectly supports games like popping balloons, catching rain, or following footprints. It invokes nostalgic, playful joy without being overly childish.
- **Patient/Therapist Relationship:** The therapist uses the panel to "open" the attractions for the day. The patient simply sees the park and the next active attraction glows and invites them in.

## Rejected Concepts
1. **The Wellness Archipelago:** (Island hopping). While relaxing, the island metaphor might limit the types of urban or abstract games we might want to add later.
2. **The Healing Garden:** (Growing plants). A strong meta-goal, but harder to visually map dynamic, fast-paced minigames (like balloon popping) into a gardening context.

## Navigation Model
- The patient arrives at the TV and sees the MOVA Plaza.
- They do not need to click complex menus. The therapist's panel dictates the current active node in the park.
- When an activity is selected by the therapist, the camera softly zooms or highlights that attraction, and the transition into the game begins.
